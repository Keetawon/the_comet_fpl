## Scoring and missingness

Use only immutable stored PMFs and exact paired identities. Player decisions are
scored at player-gameweek grain after official whole-GW finality and attachment of
every fixture leg. Partial DGWs, absent finality and missing outcomes remain
unscored with explicit counts. A descriptive dashboard may show ended matches in
an incomplete GW; that does not make the checkpoint scorable.

Reuse the existing validate-layer CRPS, log score with probability floor `1e-12`,
signed-points MAE/bias, within-GW Spearman, position slices, top-10/25/50 and captain
top-1/3/5 diagnostics. Signed errors use official `total_points_as_recorded`.
Proper-score targets sum each fixture's existing `[0,34]` coarsened target; a DGW
does not clamp its whole signed total to 34. Its stored convolved PMF is preserved.
Replayed `points_under_rules_2026_27` is a separate measure, with disagreements
reported rather than silently substituted. Team sides are deduplicated by exact
fixture/team identity. Clean-sheet probability remains opponent goal-PMF mass zero.

Raw forecast scoring and any availability-adjusted decision measures remain
separate. Missing component sidecars are NULL with reasons; do not reconstruct
forecasts to obtain them. Existing forensic labels are descriptive and noncausal.
Use paired common observations and the existing GW-block uncertainty method;
report the number of finalized GWs, never claim player rows are independent weeks.

Reports bind the selection contract, forecast hashes and outcome/finality source
identities. Identical input reporting is deterministic and idempotent. Changed
evidence needs a new report path/version; prior reports are retained. Existing
authoritative append-only outcome attachment continues to reject conflicting
corrections. Reporting does not bypass that contract or overwrite predictions.

Until eligible outcomes exist the status is **PENDING**, with uncomputed metrics.
No numerical promotion threshold is added. The existing qualitative policy is to
measure the frozen system through GW4–8 and let the owner review proper scores,
calibration, ranking, missingness and operational coverage together. This addendum
is not a preregistered statistical promotion gate.
