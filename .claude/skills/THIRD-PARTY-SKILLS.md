# Third-party skills provenance

The `ui-ux-pro-max`, `ui-styling`, and `design-system` skills are vendored unmodified from
<https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill> (MIT license, see that repository's
`LICENSE`). They carry their own scripts and data catalogs and are used for dashboard/UI design
work only; they override nothing in `AGENTS.md` (the four `*-fpl-*` skills remain authoritative
for data-correctness work). Skipped from the same repository as out of scope: `design`,
`banner-design`, `brand`, `slides` (logo/banner/brand-voice/slide-deck generation that depends on
skills not present here).
